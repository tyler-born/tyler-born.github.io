HALE: Nosecone CFD-to-FEA Verification

Ran a supersonic CFD simulation in ANSYS Fluent to obtain the pressure load on the nosecone, then applied that load in a static structural FEA of a 3D printed section. Material properties were deliberately conservative to build in margin.

Peak von Mises stress was about 1.2 MPa and maximum tip deflection was about 0.1 mm. The factor of safety stayed above 15 across the entire part.

HALE: Canard Moment Analysis

Ran supersonic CFD on a canard for an active control research project, at its maximum deflection angle and the vehicle’s top speed, to determine the maximum aerodynamic moment on the canard.

The maximum moment about the servo axis was 0.054 N·m at 5° deflection and Mach 1.1, on a canard with a 50 mm base chord.

HALE: Carbon Fiber Tube Cutting Jig

Built a jig from a repurposed 3D printer frame for cutting carbon fiber tube.

Rotating rollers lock one axis of rotation depending on the operation, and a roller on the Z-axis bar clamps and rotates the tube. A mounting arm holds a Dremel that can cut radially or travel along the tube, producing slots as well as full circumferential cuts.

Rotating the rollers and the Dremel arm by 90° gives a second configuration.

5-Arm Linkage Robotic Pen Plotter

Designed and built a robotic pen plotter around a 5-arm linkage, with two stepper motors driving the arms and a servo lifting the pen.

Designs are drawn in Inkscape and exported as G-code. A Python parser reads the G-code and uses gradient descent to convert each Cartesian point into the two motor angles, minimizing the difference between the target position and the position the linkage reaches.

The linkage can reach a given point with more than one set of joint angles. The first guess is placed in the middle of the drawing area to select the correct solution, and each later solve starts from the previous result, so it converges on the nearest, and therefore correct, solution. The parser also subdivides long moves so the pen path stays linear despite the non-linear kinematics.

Belt tension is built into the mechanical design, and a 3:1 pulley ratio increases torque at the arms.

PCB CNC Machine

Built a CNC machine on an Ender 3 frame for isolation routing of copper-clad PCBs and cutting acrylic and other plastics.

Configured a custom Marlin build with contact-based Z probing and bilinear bed leveling, tuned spindle speed and feed rate for clean isolation cuts, and rewired the Z endstop to level automatically on conductive material. The flex shaft spindle is mounted on a PETG-CF printed bracket and driven by a 1500 W inline AC speed controller.

Smart Plug Controller

A battery-powered wall button that toggles smart plugs. It is built around an ESP32-C3 with four mechanical switches and stays in deep sleep until a button press wakes it to send an HTTP request to the target plug, which gives weeks of battery life between charges.

The PCB was milled on the CNC machine above, and the back panel is machined acrylic cut on the same machine.

Competition Model Rocket

Designed, built, and launched a model rocket for a team competition, with design work focused on apogee accuracy, flight stability, and recovery success. The team won the competition.

The rocket flew on a D12-5 engine with a 3D printed nosecone and fins and an Estes parachute recovery system. A custom payload with an IMU records acceleration, angular velocity, orientation, and temperature, and is housed in a PETG-CF enclosure.

Contributions included most of the 3D design, soldering the payload electronics, and printing most of the parts.

3D Printed Mechanical Clock

Designed and assembled a weight-driven mechanical clock with a Graham escapement. Gear ratios and escapement dynamics were calculated by hand, and the finished clock keeps time to within ±3 minutes per day.

Structural parts are 3D printed, and the hardware is bearings, screws, and nuts.

Competition Drone with Electromagnet Claw

Designed a drone for a high school competition with a servo-actuated claw. The three claw arms retract back and up to expose an electromagnet at the drone’s center, so the same vehicle can grip mechanically or pick up ferrous payloads magnetically.

Completed all design work independently.

Fold N Hold

Designed a fly fishing product that holds a fully rigged rod folded to half length for transport. It is 3D printed in carbon fiber reinforced PETG, fits 3 to 7 weight rods, and ships with interchangeable tips in three sizes.

The product sells profitably and has a provisional patent application on file. 25% of profits are donated to fly-fishing nonprofits and fisheries.

Ergo Split Keyboard

Designed an ergonomic split keyboard on commission, with wrist rests and a numpad in the middle. The left half, numpad, and right half connect through magnetic snap wire connections, so each section can be repositioned independently.
